Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Introduction

Wedawon ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information across all our apps (including PartnerUp and Wedawon Language Learning, referred to as "the Apps").

🔒 Summary: We collect minimal data to provide our services. We do not sell your data to third parties. You can delete your account at any time.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials (handled securely by Firebase Authentication).
  • Content: Information you create inside the Apps — posts, messages, comments, profile bio, and any other content you submit.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the Apps, including which features you use, session duration, and progress within the Apps.
  • Device Information: Basic device details like device model, OS version, and unique identifiers, used for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Crash Reports: Crash logs and error reports to help us identify and fix issues.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the Service: To deliver app features, track progress, and personalize your experience.
  • Improve Our Apps: To analyze usage patterns and improve performance, features, and user experience.
  • Communicate: To send important updates, notifications, and respond to your inquiries.
  • Security: To protect our Apps and users from fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect and process your data:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for authentication, cloud storage, analytics, and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution and in-app updates.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase's cloud infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or deletion.

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols.
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to user data, and only for legitimate business purposes.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can update or correct your personal information through the App settings.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account directly inside the App (Settings → Delete My Account). Alternatively, contact us at the email below.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of non-essential data collection through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date above.
